# Orqentra guide · Human intervention and support handoff
To talk to a human in Orqentra, open a conversation in Chat, select Request human help and then Confirm request. Confirmation shares this conversation and its history with your organization's OWNER/ADMIN support team. Other private conversations stay private. AI replies pause while the request is waiting or claimed.

WAITING means no human has claimed the conversation yet. A requester can leave a message while waiting. A separate real user with an active OWNER/ADMIN membership opens Chat's Human support inbox, selects the request and chooses Claim conversation. Only the assigned support teammate may send support replies. A requester cannot claim their own request. Demo profiles cannot answer or sign in.

The requester or assigned teammate can choose Return to AI. Human history is preserved for the requester, but staff access to someone else's private chat closes. Human replies do not automatically become approved RAG knowledge. Updates use polling, and there is no guaranteed response time or automatic email/Slack notification. A real support person must be available.
